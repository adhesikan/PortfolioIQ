ABUSE PREVENTION
-----------------------------------------

Log:
- Hashed IP (sha256 + salt)
- deviceId (UUID in httpOnly cookie)
- user agent
- timestamp

Store in AbuseLog table.

Do NOT block by IP alone.
Instead:
- Increase risk score
- Require verification or CAPTCHA if suspicious.
